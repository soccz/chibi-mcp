# Code of Conduct

This project is for practical, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issues and discussions on topic.
- Respect privacy and do not post secrets, private logs, or personal data.
- Do not submit copyrighted character art, brand assets, or NSFW content as character packs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Spam or low-effort promotion.
- Publicly posting exploit details for unresolved vulnerabilities.
- Submitting assets you do not have rights to use.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that harms the project or its contributors.
